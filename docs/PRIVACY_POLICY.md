# Privacy Policy for WMeal

**Last Updated: November 28, 2025**

## Introduction

WMeal ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our meal planning service.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- **Account Information**: Email address, name (if provided)
- **Authentication Data**: Encrypted passwords managed by Firebase Authentication
- **Profile Data**: Dietary preferences, serving sizes, allergies, health goals

### Usage Information
- **Meal Plans**: Saved meal plans, recipes, and grocery lists
- **Activity Data**: Features used, generation frequency, search queries
- **Device Information**: IP address, browser type, device type, operating system

### Automatically Collected Information
- Cookies and similar tracking technologies
- Analytics data for service improvement

## How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:
- Provide and maintain the meal planning service
- Generate personalized meal plans using AI
- Save and retrieve your meal plans
- Improve user experience and service functionality
- Send important service notifications
- Analyze usage patterns for service optimization
- Prevent fraud and ensure security

## Data Storage and Security

- **Authentication**: Handled by Firebase Authentication with industry-standard security
- **Database**: Meal plans stored in Vercel Postgres with encryption at rest
- **API Communications**: All data transmitted via HTTPS
- **Access Control**: User data isolated and accessible only to the account owner

## Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:
- **Firebase**: Authentication and user management
- **Google Gemini AI**: Meal plan and recipe generation (no personal data stored)
- **Vercel**: Hosting and database services
- **Analytics**: Vercel Analytics for usage statistics

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your account and associated data
- Export your meal plans
- Opt-out of marketing communications
- Withdraw consent at any time

## Data Retention

- Account data retained until account deletion
- Meal plans stored indefinitely unless deleted by user
- Analytics data aggregated and anonymized after 90 days
- Deleted accounts purged within 30 days

## Children's Privacy

WMeal is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## International Users

Data is processed and stored in servers located in Australia and the United States. By using our service, you consent to data transfer to these locations.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Users will be notified of significant changes via email or in-app notification.
